The crule feature records the clause rule, derived from the XML attribute Rule, on syntactic constituents in the Nestle 1904 Greek New Testament. It is a node feature with exactly two observed values: ClCl (8,616 occurrences) and ClCl2 (2,500 occurrences), totaling 11,116 annotated nodes. Sources are distributed across clause (4,720), sentence (838), and wg (5,558) nodes. Representative clause examples show ClCl2 and ClCl attached to Matthew 1 birth-narrative text. The semantic distinction between ClCl and ClCl2, and the relationship of crule to other clause-classification features such as clausetype and cltype, are not documented in the supplied evidence; interpretation remains tentative.

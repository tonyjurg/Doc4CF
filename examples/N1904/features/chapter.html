The chapter feature is a Text-Fabric node feature recording the chapter number from the ref attribute in the XML. It exists on chapter nodes (260) and is projected to clause, group, phrase, sentence, subphrase, verse, wg, and word nodes, supporting navigation in the book/chapter/verse reference system alongside book and verse. The corpus shows 28 distinct chapter numbers. Frequent values include chapter 1 (42,442 nodes), chapter 2 (35,630), chapter 3 (31,095), chapter 4 (31,176), chapter 5 (28,180), chapter 6 (24,499), chapter 7 (22,640), chapter 8 (21,510), chapter 9 (23,000), chapter 10 (21,100), chapter 11 (22,533), and chapter 12 (22,364). Chapter numbers restart within each book, so chapter is not unique without book context.

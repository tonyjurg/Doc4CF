Open questions

These unresolved points may need editorial review before the descriptions are treated as settled.

Show open questions
  • application.configuration.writing is 'grc', but feature lang has one distinct value 'el' observed only on book nodes; the intended language tag for the corpus text is unclear.
  • features.clausetype and features.cltype are both described as 'clause type' but have different value sets (clausetype: only 'nominalized'; cltype: VerbElided/Verbless/Minor); their relationship is undocumented.
  • features.oslots and features.subjref are edges with distinct_values 0 and empty examples yet non-empty sources/targets, an internal inconsistency needing review.
  • Multiple word surface representations coexist (text, normalized, unaccent, translit, unicode, plus before/after/trailer) without a stated authoritative form for citation.
  • features.after and features.trailer both describe post-word material (trailer excludes critical signs); the distinction is not fully clarified.
  • features.domain and features.ln cover similar node sets with numeric-looking values but no documented relationship.

The vs feature is a node-level morphological tag recording verbal stem on word nodes in the Dead Sea Scrolls corpus. Values are populated on word nodes; unattested words have no recorded value rather than a negative stem assignment. Attested stems include qal and hifil among 41 distinct values, with one value marking cases where the stem is not identified.

Presence/absence associations

Each comparison is calculated separately across all nodes of the listed type. Present means the feature has an assigned value; absent means no assignment. φ ranges from −1 (opposite presence patterns) to +1 (matching patterns). Only non-constant pairs with at least 10 present and 10 absent nodes per feature, and |φ| ≥ 0.10, are shown. Identical presence patterns are grouped.

The lex node feature records the Unicode representation of a lexeme on lex and word nodes in the Dead Sea Scrolls corpus. sources counts populated nodes by type, so unassigned word nodes have no recorded value rather than a negative class. The lex display also uses lexe and the bare feature lexo; occ links lexemes to occurrences.

Presence/absence associations

Each comparison is calculated separately across all nodes of the listed type. Present means the feature has an assigned value; absent means no assignment. φ ranges from −1 (opposite presence patterns) to +1 (matching patterns). Only non-constant pairs with at least 10 present and 10 absent nodes per feature, and |φ| ≥ 0.10, are shown. Identical presence patterns are grouped. These are descriptive associations, not causal claims. Read the scoring method.

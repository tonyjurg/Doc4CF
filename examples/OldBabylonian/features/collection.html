The collection feature is a document-level node attribute in the AbB Old Babylonian Cuneiform corpus that records the modern publication or museum collection to which a cuneiform document belongs. It has 17 distinct values across 1,284 documented sources, with AbB most frequent (492), followed by CT (241), VS (218), YOS (108), TCL (105), LIH (77), and smaller sets including YNER, TLB, BIN, OECT, AJSL, and CT43,. One document lacks a collection value, and the corpus label “AbB” does not exclusively match the feature’s contents.

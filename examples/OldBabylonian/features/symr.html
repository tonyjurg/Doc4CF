symr is a node feature in the AbB Old Babylonian Cuneiform Text-Fabric corpus, recording the essential part of a sign or word using non-ASCII characters. It is sourced on 203,219 sign nodes and 76,505 word nodes, with 16,805 distinct values. Frequent values include x, a, ma, na, ša, u₃, i, šu, d, a-na, li, and am. The feature belongs to the Sign and Word Essential Forms family alongside atf, sym, symu, after, and afterr, and appears in the text-orig-rich rendering format ({symr}{afterr}). Its exact relationship to sym and symu, including normalization rules, is tentative and unspecified.

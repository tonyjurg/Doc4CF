Its edges express slot membership; they do not by themselves identify semantic, syntactic, or parent-child relationships between non-slot units.document, face, line, word, cluster
otextconfigConfigures Text-Fabric's Text API. Depending on the corpus, its settings can define section levels and their identifying features, text formats, and formatting templates. Text-Fabric uses this configuration to locate and render text for nodes. It is configuration rather than a node- or edge-valued feature, so its entries describe how the corpus is presented, not annotations attached to individual nodes.

AbB Old Babylonian Cuneiform

Dataset architecture and glossary

Model-generated interpretation. AbB Old Babylonian Cuneiform is a Text-Fabric corpus of 1,285 cuneiform documents organized as document > face > line > word > cluster > sign, with sign as the slot type; reference sections are document, face, and line using section features pnumber, face, and lnno. It contains 203,219 signs and includes ATF transcriptions, readings, graphemes, sign-status flags, languages (Akkadian and Sumerian), periods, genres, and museum metadata.
